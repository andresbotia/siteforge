# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-09-06

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** SiteForge builds independent restaurants a finished, working website and shows it to them before asking for anything.
**What it does:** SiteForge finds local restaurants with outdated, template, or missing websites, builds a complete replacement site for them first, and sends the owner a live preview link. If they like it, setup is a flat $99 one-time fee (covers the build, domain registration, and the first year of hosting), with continued management at $39/month optional after that.
**Product category:** Done-for-you small business website service (not a DIY builder, not a template marketplace, not an agency retainer).
**Product type:** Service, delivered by a real person, backed by internal software tooling (not sold as software).
**Business model:** One-time setup fee ($99) + optional recurring management ($39/mo after year one). No contract, no lock-in — domain transfers to the business on request at any time.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Independent, owner-operated restaurants in Broward County, FL. Not chains, not franchises, not businesses with an in-house marketing person or an agency already on retainer.
**Decision-makers:** The owner or general manager — usually the same person who currently maintains (or neglects) the website, if one exists at all.
**Primary use case:** Replace a website that is actively costing the business calls, reservations, and covers because it looks unmaintained, isn't mobile-friendly, or doesn't show the menu clearly.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Stop losing walk-ins/reservations to a competitor whose site looks more current
- Make the menu visible and readable on a phone in two taps or fewer
- Have a website that "just works" without the owner touching a website builder ever again
**Use cases:**
- Restaurant has no standalone website at all (Facebook page or nothing)
- Restaurant has an old, template, or agency-abandoned site that looks dated
- Restaurant's site exists but the menu is a PDF, an image, or missing on mobile

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Their current website (or lack of one) is quietly turning away business — people check on their phone, can't find the menu or hours, and go somewhere else instead.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- DIY builders (Wix/Squarespace) require time and design judgment the owner doesn't have and won't spend
- Marketplace templates look generic and dated within a year, with no one maintaining them
- Traditional agencies quote a large upfront fee for a promise and a mockup, not a finished product
**What it costs them:** Missed calls, missed reservations, and covers lost to the competitor down the street with a better-looking site — invisible attrition that never shows up as a complaint.
**Emotional tension:** Embarrassment that the site looks unmaintained; skepticism from having been burned by agencies before; reluctance to spend more time or money without seeing something real first.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Local web design freelancers/agencies — fall short because they sell a promise and a deposit, not a finished site, and disappear after launch.
**Secondary:** DIY builders (Wix, Squarespace, Google Sites) — fall short because they shift the ongoing time and design burden back onto an owner who has neither.
**Indirect:** Doing nothing / relying on a Facebook or Instagram page as "the website" — falls short because it doesn't show a real menu, doesn't rank in search the same way, and looks like the business isn't running its own show.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- We build the finished site FIRST, before any ask — proof, not a pitch
- Flat, fixed price ($99) covering the build, domain, and a full year of hosting — no surprise line items
- A real, local person handles it end to end, not a marketplace of freelancers or a self-serve tool
- No lock-in: the domain is registered in the business's name and transfers on request at any time, whether or not they keep the monthly plan
**How we do it differently:** Instead of asking for a meeting or a deposit, we do the work speculatively and hand over a live, working preview link.
**Why that's better:** The owner is judging a finished thing, not imagining one from a mockup or a sales call — removes the two biggest objections (will it actually look good, and will they actually deliver).
**Why customers choose us:** They can see and click through the exact site they'd be buying before spending a dollar.

## Objections & Anti-Personas
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "How do I know this isn't a scam / low-effort template?" | The preview link is the answer — it's not a mockup or a pitch, it's the actual finished site running live. |
| "What happens if I stop paying the $39/month?" | Nothing owed if you never start it. If it lapses after being active, the site stays up 30 days, then comes down — either way, you keep the site files and the domain transfers to you. |
| "Is this locked in / will you own my domain?" | The domain is registered in your business's name from day one; SiteForge is only listed as the technical contact and transfers it to you on request, anytime. |
**Anti-persona:** Chains, franchises, or restaurants with an in-house marketing hire or an existing agency relationship — they don't feel the core pain (no time/no skill to maintain a site) and are not the target of cold outreach.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** A site that looks unmaintained is an active, ongoing embarrassment and a believed source of lost business, even if the owner can't quantify it.
**Pull:** Seeing a finished, working replacement site for their exact business, for a flat and modest price, with zero upfront commitment.
**Habit:** Inertia — "the current site is fine" or "I'll get to it eventually" — plus fatigue from a past bad experience with an agency or DIY builder.
**Anxiety:** Fear of being oversold, locked into a contract, or losing control of their own domain to a vendor.
